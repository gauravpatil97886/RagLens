# Acme Innotech Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Handbook

**Effective date:** 1 April 2025
**Applies to:** All full-time employees of Acme Innotech Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company"), across all office locations in India. Interns, consultants, and contractors are covered by separate agreements and are outside the scope of this handbook unless a section explicitly says otherwise.

This handbook is a summary of Company policy. It does not override your signed offer letter or employment agreement; where the two conflict, the employment agreement governs. HR (hr@acmeinnotech.example) is the point of contact for any clarification.

---

## 1. Employment Classification and Probation

Every new hire joins as a probationary employee. The standard probation period is **90 days** from the date of joining. During this period either party may end the employment relationship with the notice described in Section 7.

At the end of 90 days, your manager and HR will review your performance. If performance is satisfactory, you are confirmed in writing and probation ends. If performance is not yet satisfactory but shows promise, probation may be **extended once, by up to 30 additional days**, with written justification from your manager. Probation may not be extended a second time — at the end of the extension the employee is either confirmed or separated.

Employees are classified as **exempt** (salaried, not eligible for overtime) or **non-exempt** (hourly, overtime-eligible) as stated in their offer letter.

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

Standard working hours are **9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday**, which includes a one-hour lunch break, for a 9-hour on-site day / 8-hour working day. Core collaboration hours, during which every employee (remote or on-site) must be reachable, are **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST**.

Non-exempt employees who work beyond standard hours at their manager's request are paid overtime at **1.5x their hourly rate**, calculated on basic pay. Exempt employees do not receive overtime pay; sustained excess hours should instead be flagged to HR as a staffing or workload issue.

Attendance is logged through the internal portal. Three or more unexplained absences in a rolling 30-day window trigger an automatic HR check-in.

## 3. Leave Policy

Acme's leave year runs from **1 April to 31 March**. All leave balances reset on 1 April; unused leave in categories other than Earned Leave lapses at year end and is not carried forward or encashed.

- **Casual Leave (CL):** 8 days per year, for short personal matters. Cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days without manager approval in advance.
- **Sick Leave (SL):** 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days.
- **Earned/Privilege Leave (EL):** 15 days per year, accrued monthly at **1.25 days per month**. EL can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of **45 days**. On resignation or termination, unused EL is encashed at your last-drawn basic salary rate, up to a maximum of **30 days**.
- **Maternity Leave:** 26 weeks (182 days), in line with the Maternity Benefit Act, for the first two children. A third child onward is eligible for 12 weeks.
- **Paternity Leave:** 10 working days, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.
- **Bereavement Leave:** 5 days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), 2 days for extended family.
- **Leave Without Pay (LWP):** May be granted by a manager in exceptional cases once CL and SL are exhausted, capped at 15 days per year without HR escalation.

Leave requests go through the portal and require manager approval before the leave date, except for sick leave, which may be applied for on the day of absence.

## 4. Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Confirmed employees (i.e., those who have completed probation) may work remotely up to **2 days per week**, subject to manager approval and team scheduling needs. Employees on probation are expected to work on-site full time unless their offer letter states otherwise, so that onboarding and mentorship happen in person.

Fully remote arrangements (more than 2 days a week) require sign-off from a Vice President and are reviewed every 6 months. Regardless of remote schedule, every employee must be reachable during core hours (10:00 AM–4:00 PM IST, see Section 2) and must attend any meeting their manager marks as "in-person required" with at least 3 business days' notice.

Employees approved for regular remote work receive a **one-time home office stipend of ₹15,000**, reimbursed against receipts for desk, chair, monitor, or similar equipment, claimable once every 3 years.

## 5. Travel and Expense Reimbursement

All business travel must be approved by your manager before booking. The following limits apply to domestic travel:

- **Meals per diem:** ₹1,500 per day.
- **Hotel cap:** ₹4,500 per night in metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune); ₹3,000 per night elsewhere.
- **Local conveyance:** reimbursed at actuals with receipts; claims under ₹500 may be submitted without a receipt but must include a short note on purpose.
- **International travel:** business class is not authorized below Director level; economy class and a per diem set by HR on a per-trip basis apply otherwise.

Employees who are required to be on-call or reachable outside working hours receive a **monthly mobile and internet reimbursement of ₹1,000**, claimed alongside regular expenses.

**Reimbursement timeline:** Expense claims must be submitted within **15 days** of the expense being incurred. Managers must approve or query a claim within **3 working days** of submission. Once approved, Finance disburses reimbursement within **7 working days**. Claims submitted more than 30 days after the expense was incurred will not be honored except with VP approval.

## 6. Notice Period and Separation

Notice period depends on employment stage and level:

- **During probation:** 30 days' notice from either side, or payment of basic salary in lieu of notice.
- **After confirmation, individual contributor:** 60 days' notice.
- **After confirmation, manager and above:** 90 days' notice.

An employee may buy out part or all of their notice period by paying the Company an amount equal to their basic salary for the unserved portion; this requires manager and HR approval and is not automatic.

On separation, the employee must return all Company assets (laptop, access card, SIM, any hardware) within **5 working days** of their last working day. Full and final settlement — including salary for days worked, EL encashment (Section 3), and any pending reimbursements — is processed within **45 days** of the last working day.

Gratuity is paid as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, to employees who complete **5 years of continuous service**, calculated at 15 days' wages for every completed year of service.

## 7. Hiring, Referrals, and Background Checks

All offers are contingent on a background check, which must be cleared within **30 days of joining**; failure to clear it may result in termination of employment during probation.

Employees who refer a successful candidate receive a **referral bonus of ₹25,000**, paid out after the referred candidate completes **90 days** of employment (i.e., their probation period), provided both referrer and referred employee are still active at that time.

## 8. Performance Reviews and Compensation

Formal performance reviews happen **twice a year, in April and October**. Compensation increments, when awarded, are effective **1 April** each year and are communicated by the end of April. Promotions are considered during the April review cycle; the October cycle is feedback-only and does not carry compensation changes.

## 9. IT, Data Security, and Confidentiality

Company laptops are encrypted by default and must not be modified to disable disk encryption. Passwords must be rotated every **90 days** and must not be reused across the last 5 passwords. Personal USB storage devices may not be connected to Company laptops. Any suspected security incident (lost device, phishing attempt, unauthorized access) must be reported to IT Security within **24 hours** of discovery.

All work product, source code, and confidential business information created during employment belongs to the Company, and this obligation survives termination of employment.

## 10. Grievance Redressal

Any employee with a workplace concern — regarding a manager, a peer, compensation, or policy application — may raise it with HR directly, or anonymously through the ethics portal. HR will acknowledge a grievance within **3 working days** and aims to close it within **21 working days**, though complex cases (e.g., formal harassment complaints, which follow the separate POSH policy) may take longer and follow statutory timelines instead of this general one.
